Does La Niña Actually Matter for Colorado Snowpack?

Short answer: no. Knowing whether it is a La Niña year does not tell you if Colorado's snow will be good or bad.

46 winters of federal SNOTEL data and NOAA's Oceanic Niño Index say: barely, and not significantly.
